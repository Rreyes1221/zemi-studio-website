Before your appointment

Everything you need for the best results.

A few thoughtful steps will help you feel comfortable and allow us to give your brows and lashes the care they deserve.

01

Arrive with clean brows & lashes

Please avoid mascara, brow makeup, or heavy eye makeup.

02

Skip oil-based products

Avoid oils, balms, or heavy skincare around the eye area for 24 hours.

03

Remove contact lenses

Please take them out before your appointment if receiving a lash service.

04

Wear your hair down

Loose hair allows for a more comfortable treatment.

05

Avoid strong fragrances

Many guests are sensitive to strong scents.

06

Running late?

Please text us as soon as possible so we can accommodate you if we’re able.

Meet your esthetician

Meet Heidi.
The face behind Zemi Studio.

Hi, I’m Heidi! I’m a licensed esthetician and the face behind Zemi Studio. I’ve always had a passion for beauty and helping others feel confident in their own skin, which inspired me to turn that passion into a career.

I specialize in brows and lashes, with a focus on enhancing your natural beauty through personalized treatments. Whether you’re here for a simple brow shaping or a complete brow and lash transformation, my goal is to make sure you leave feeling beautiful and confident.

I created Zemi Studio to be a welcoming space where you can relax, feel comfortable, and enjoy a little time for yourself. I’m so excited to continue growing, meeting new faces, and building lasting relationships with my clients.

I can’t wait to welcome you to Zemi Studio!
